## Part 3: Reading Comprehension (1 קטע + 5 שאלות, ~8 דקות)

> **The Sleep-Learning Connection**
>
> Most people think of sleep as a period of rest, but research over the past decade has shown that it is also a time of intense brain activity. In particular, scientists have discovered that sleep plays a crucial role in learning and memory. While we sleep, the brain reviews and organizes the information we gathered during the day, strengthening important connections and discarding unnecessary ones.
>
> Studies at several universities have demonstrated that people who sleep well after learning something new remember it much better than those who stay awake. In one famous experiment, students who studied vocabulary and then slept for eight hours remembered about 35 percent more words the next day compared to students who studied the same words but stayed awake all night. This finding has changed how many educators and doctors think about the value of rest.
>
> However, not all sleep is equally helpful. The deepest stage of sleep, called slow-wave sleep, appears to be especially important for strengthening memories of facts and events. REM sleep - the stage when most dreaming occurs - seems more connected to processing emotions and creative problem-solving. Both stages are needed for full mental recovery, which is why a short nap, while refreshing, cannot fully replace a complete night of sleep.
>
> Given these findings, experts recommend getting at least seven to nine hours of sleep every night, especially during periods of intensive learning such as exam weeks. Unfortunately, many students do exactly the opposite - staying up late to study and then struggling to remember what they read. The science is clear: if you want to learn effectively, sleeping is not optional.

## 📝 Part 7: פירוט התשובות (להבנה לאחר המבחן)

### Question 1: **(C) in**
**"I usually drink coffee in the morning."**
מילת יחס לזמן של חלק ביום: **in the morning / afternoon / evening**. אבל: **at night**.

### Question 2: **(B) goes**
**"She goes to the gym three times a week."**
Subject-verb agreement: `She` (גוף שלישי יחיד) + `goes` (עם -s).

### Question 3: **(B) wanted**
**"Despite the rain, the children wanted to play outside."**
`Despite` הוא ניגוד. הילדים **רוצים** לשחק למרות הגשם. `refused` היה הפוך מניגוד, `forgot` ו-`stopped` לא הגיוניים בהקשר.

### Question 4: **(B) conducted**
**"The research project was conducted by a team of experts..."**
`conduct research` = לערוך מחקר. זו collocation קבועה באנגלית אקדמית.

### Question 5: **(A) bring about**
**"The new regulations will bring about significant changes..."**
`bring about` = phrasal verb שמשמעותו "לגרום ל-" / "להביא ל-".

### Question 6: **(C) despite being**
**"The novel, despite being popular at first, has now become a classic..."**
`despite being + adjective` = מבנה concessive. "אף שהיה פופולרי בהתחלה" – הקטע ממשיך ל"הפך לקלאסיקה".

### Question 7: **(C) Tom's sister is older than Tom**
"Tom is younger than his sister" = "His sister is older than Tom". היפוך נקודת המבט.

### Question 8: **(C) She moved to Paris three years ago and still lives there**
Present Perfect Continuous (`has been living for X years`) = פעולה שהתחילה בעבר וממשיכה להווה.

### Question 9: **(B) I do not have enough money to buy a new car**
Second Conditional (`If I had... I would...`) = מצב דמיוני בהווה. רומז על ההפך במציאות.

### Question 10: **(B) Because the professor explained the concept clearly, everyone grasped it right away**
`so X that Y` (סיבה-תוצאה) = `Because X, Y`. "grasp" = "understand".

### Question 11: **(C) It reviews and organizes information from the day**
פסקה 1: "the brain reviews and organizes the information we gathered during the day".

### Question 12: **(B) Students who slept remembered about 35 percent more words**
פסקה 2: "remembered about 35 percent more words the next day compared to students who... stayed awake".

### Question 13: **(C) strengthening memories of facts and events**
פסקה 3: "slow-wave sleep, appears to be especially important for strengthening memories of facts and events".

### Question 14: **(B) Sleep is required, not a luxury**
"not optional" = "is required". הטון הוא שסגירת עיניים היא **הכרחית**, לא בחירה חופשית.

### Question 15: **(B) Sleep plays an important role in learning and memory, and is essential for effective study**
הקטע בכללו על הקשר בין שינה ללמידה, עם המסקנה שזה חיוני.
