### 🔹 פרק 3 – Reading Comprehension (5 שאלות, 15 דקות)

#### 📖 Passage: The Psychology of Decision Fatigue

> Every day, we make thousands of decisions - from small choices like what to eat for breakfast to significant ones involving our careers and relationships. While this may seem like a simple aspect of daily life, psychologists have discovered that decision-making is not a limitless resource. A phenomenon known as "decision fatigue" describes how the quality of our decisions tends to deteriorate after a long session of choosing. This finding has important implications for how we organize our lives, work, and even our legal systems.
>
> The concept gained widespread attention after a famous 2011 study of Israeli parole judges. Researchers found that prisoners appearing before judges early in the day had a roughly 65 percent chance of being granted parole, while those whose hearings occurred just before a meal break faced a chance of nearly zero. After the break, the favorable ruling rate jumped back up. The researchers concluded that as judges made more decisions throughout the morning, their mental energy depleted, pushing them toward the "safer" default option of denying parole. This was not a matter of bias or corruption, but of fatigue.
>
> Further research has shown that decision fatigue affects almost everyone, though often in subtle ways. Shoppers who spend long periods comparing products tend to make poorer final choices than those who decide more quickly. Doctors at the end of a long shift are more likely to prescribe unnecessary antibiotics than those earlier in their shift. Even judgments about what to eat are affected: tired decision-makers gravitate toward sugary, high-calorie foods - a preference that helps explain why willpower often weakens later in the day.
>
> Some successful leaders have reportedly tried to avoid decision fatigue by minimizing trivial choices. Both Barack Obama and Steve Jobs were known for wearing essentially the same outfit every day, freeing mental energy for more important matters. While such extreme strategies are not practical for everyone, researchers suggest simpler approaches: making important decisions early in the day, creating routines that automate minor choices, and taking short breaks during demanding decision-making sessions.
>
> Understanding decision fatigue does not mean we must avoid decisions altogether - an impossible goal in modern life. Rather, it suggests that we should treat our mental resources with the same care we give to physical ones. Just as an athlete plans training around periods of rest, effective decision-makers might benefit from planning their choices around natural cycles of mental energy.

## 📝 Part 2: Answer Key and Explanations

### 🔹 פרק 1

#### ✅ Q1 – **(A) rapid**

"Remarkably successful expansion" → התרחבות שהצליחה מאוד לרוב מתאפיינת ב**מהירות** (rapid expansion = Collocation נפוץ בעולם העסקי).

- ❌ `slow` סותר את ההצלחה המהירה.
- ❌ `reluctant`, `hesitant` = היסוס - הפוך מיוזמה להרחיב.

**💡 טיפ:** `rapid expansion`, `rapid growth` = Collocations עסקיים חשובים.

---

#### ✅ Q2 – **(B) scheduling**

"Couldn't attend because of a ___ conflict" → Collocation קבוע: `scheduling conflict` = התנגשות בלוחות זמנים.

- ❌ `hopeful`, `grateful`, `amusing` - לא יוצרים Collocation סביר עם "conflict".

---

#### ✅ Q3 – **(B) compelling**

"Left no room for doubt" - רמז חזק: הראיות היו **משכנעות** לחלוטין.

- ✅ `compelling evidence` = ראיות משכנעות. Collocation חשוב.
- ❌ `questionable`, `confusing`, `missing` - סותרים "no doubt".

**💡 טיפ:** `compelling evidence/argument/reason` - ביטויים אקדמיים-משפטיים נפוצים.

---

#### ✅ Q4 – **(B) balance**

"Balance work with family" = לאזן בין עבודה ומשפחה. Collocation מוכר.

- ❌ `damage`, `destroy` שליליים.
- ❌ `celebrate` לא הגיוני עם "struggle".

---

### 🔹 פרק 2

#### ✅ Q5 – **(C) thorough**

"Even the most confused students understood" → הרמז: ההסבר היה **יסודי** ומפורט מספיק לכולם.

- ❌ `vague` = עמום. סותר.
- ❌ `complicated` = מסובך. סותר "finally understood".
- ✅ `thorough` = יסודי, מקיף.
- ❌ `brief` = קצר. לא מסביר לגמרי למה כולם הבינו.

---

#### ✅ Q6 – **(B) Despite**

"Limited budget" + "managed to travel to 5 countries" = **ניגוד**: למרות תקציב נמוך, הצליח להרבה נסיעות.

- ❌ `Because of`, `Due to` - סיבה.
- ❌ `Thanks to` - חיובי, אבל "limited budget" שלילי - לא מתאים.
- ✅ `Despite` = למרות. ניגוד מדויק.

---

#### ✅ Q7 – **(A) shed**

`shed light on X` = Collocation קבוע: "לשפוך אור על". זו הצורה הנכונה היחידה בצירוף זה.

- ❌ `cast`, `threw`, `spread` - לא Collocations נכונים עם "light on".

**💡 טיפ:** `shed light on` (שופך אור) = ביטוי חשוב למחקר ולדיסקבריות.

---

#### ✅ Q8 – **(C) vague**

"Criticized for being ___ on specific policy details" - מבקרים אומרים **שהיה חסר פירוט**.

- ❌ `rich`, `heavy`, `direct` - חיוביים או ניטרליים.

### 🧪 פרק 7 – Grammar in Context

#### ✅ Q24 – **(C) had studied**

**Third Conditional:** `If X had V3, Y would have V3`. "She would have passed" = וולד חיווי → התנאי חייב להיות `had studied`.

- ❌ `studies` - הווה.
- ❌ `studied` - Second Conditional (כבר היה "would pass", לא "would have passed").
- ✅ `had studied` - Third Conditional.
- ❌ `will study` - עתיד.

---

#### ✅ Q25 – **(C) whose**

"The book ___ cover is torn" - שייכות (הכיסוי שייך לספר). → `whose` (שייכות גם לחפצים, לא רק לאנשים).

- ❌ `who` - לאנשים (ולא שייכות).
- ❌ `which` - relative pronoun לחפצים, אבל לא לשייכות.
- ✅ `whose` - שייכות.
- ❌ `what` - לא relative pronoun במבנה זה.

**💡 טיפ:** `whose` חל גם על חפצים! "The book whose cover..." = "הספר שהכיסוי שלו...".

---

#### ✅ Q26 – **(B) were**

**Neither-nor rule:** הפועל מסכים עם השם **הקרוב ביותר**. כאן "employees" (רבים) קרוב יותר → `were`.

- ❌ `was` - יחיד; אם היה "Neither the employees nor the manager", אז `was`.
- ✅ `were` - רבים, לפי "employees".
- (C), (D) - הווה, לא מתאים להקשר.

**💡 טיפ:** "Neither X nor Y [V]" - הפועל מסכים עם Y (הקרוב).

---

#### ✅ Q27 – **(C) will have celebrated**

**Future Perfect:** "By next year" = נקודת זמן עתידית. לפני הזמן הזה, הפעולה תושלם.

- ❌ `celebrates` - הווה.
- ❌ `celebrated` - עבר.
- ✅ `will have celebrated` - Future Perfect.
- ❌ `has celebrated` - Present Perfect, לא מתאים עם "by next year".

**💡 טיפ:** "By + future time + will have + V3" = מבנה Future Perfect.

---

### 🧪 פרק 8 – Word Formation

#### ✅ Q28 – **(B) exceptional**

"Demonstrated ___ teamwork" - אחרי `demonstrated` + לפני שם עצם (teamwork) צריך תואר = `exceptional` (יוצא דופן).

---

#### ✅ Q29 – **(A) attention**

"Her ___ to detail" - אחרי "her" + לפני "to" צריך שם עצם = `attention`. Collocation: `attention to detail`.

---

#### ✅ Q30 – **(B) resistance**

"Met with ___ from staff" - אחרי "with" צריך שם עצם = `resistance` (התנגדות). Collocation: `met with resistance`.

---

#### ✅ Q31 – **(B) rigorous**

"___ research methodology" - לפני שם עצם (methodology) צריך תואר = `rigorous` (קפדני).

שים לב להבדל: `rigor` (קפדנות, שם עצם) vs `rigorous` (קפדני, תואר). ו-`rigid` (קשוח) זה תואר שונה לגמרי.
