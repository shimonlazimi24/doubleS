# מדריך: מבני משפט חשובים לניסוח מחדש
## Unit 5.3 – Important Sentence Structures for Restatement

**מטרה:** להכיר את 5 משפחות המבנים הדקדוקיים הכי נפוצות בשאלות Restatement, ולהבין איך לזהות ולהמיר ביניהן.

---

## 📋 תוכן עניינים

1. Active ↔ Passive Voice
2. Conditionals (0/1/2/3 + Mixed)
3. Cause & Effect (סיבה ותוצאה)
4. Concession (ניגוד)
5. Comparison (השוואה)
6. סיכום וטיפים

---
## 1️⃣ Active ↔ Passive Voice

### המבנה הבסיסי

**Active (פעיל):**
`Subject + Verb + Object`
> "The scientist discovered a new virus."

**Passive (סביל):**
`Object + to be + V3 (past participle) + (by + Subject)`
> "A new virus was discovered by the scientist."

שני המשפטים **זהים במשמעות**, רק הדגש שונה:
- Active: הדגש על **מי עשה** (the scientist).
- Passive: הדגש על **מה קרה** (a new virus was discovered).

### זמני Passive

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| She writes letters. | Letters are written (by her). |
| She is writing letters. | Letters are being written. |
| She wrote letters. | Letters were written. |
| She has written letters. | Letters have been written. |
| She will write letters. | Letters will be written. |
| She can write letters. | Letters can be written. |

### איך לזהות במבחן?

**רמזים ל-Passive:**
- צורת `to be` + `V3` (was/were eaten, is made, has been built)
- לעיתים קרובות `by + someone/something`
- הנושא לרוב לא עושה את הפעולה אלא **מקבל אותה**

### דוגמה לפרפרזה

**Original (Passive):** "The paintings were stolen by an unknown thief last night."

**Paraphrase (Active):** "An unknown thief stole the paintings last night."

### 💡 פחים נפוצים
1. **היפוך תפקידים:**
   - ❌ "The paintings stole the thief" (שגוי ומצחיק)
   - ✅ "An unknown thief stole the paintings"

2. **איבוד של "by" מידע:**
   - אם ה-Passive לא מציין **מי עשה** ("The door was locked"), ה-Active צריך להיות כללי: "Someone locked the door."

## 2️⃣ Conditionals – משפטי תנאי

### 4 הסוגים הבסיסיים

#### Zero Conditional – עובדות כלליות
**Form:** `If + Present Simple, Present Simple`
> "If you heat water to 100°C, it boils."

**משמעות:** חוק טבע, תמיד נכון.

**Paraphrase:** 
- "When you heat water to 100°C, it boils."
- "Whenever you heat water to 100°C, it boils."

---

#### First Conditional – תנאי מציאותי לעתיד
**Form:** `If + Present Simple, will + V`
> "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay home."

**משמעות:** תנאי **סביר** בעתיד. לא יודעים אם יקרה.

**Paraphrase:** 
- "Unless it rains tomorrow, I won't stay home."
- "Should it rain tomorrow, I will stay home." (Formal, with inversion)

**מציאות:** לא ידועה. אולי כן, אולי לא.

---

#### Second Conditional – תנאי דמיוני בהווה
**Form:** `If + Past Simple, would + V`
> "If I had more money, I would buy a new car."

**משמעות:** מצב **לא-מציאותי** או **דמיוני** בהווה.

**מציאות המובלעת:** אין לי מספיק כסף, ולכן אני לא קונה רכב.

**Paraphrase המציאות:**
- "I don't have enough money, so I can't buy a new car."
- "Were I richer, I would buy a new car." (Formal inversion)

---

#### Third Conditional – תנאי דמיוני בעבר
**Form:** `If + Past Perfect (had + V3), would have + V3`
> "If she had studied harder, she would have passed the exam."

**משמעות:** מצב **דמיוני בעבר** – מה שלא קרה, אבל היה יכול לקרות.

**מציאות המובלעת:** היא לא למדה מספיק, ולכן נכשלה.

**Paraphrase:**
- "She did not study hard enough, so she failed."
- "Had she studied harder, she would have passed." (Formal inversion)

---

#### Mixed Conditional – תנאי מעורב
**Form:** `If + Past Perfect, would + V` (תנאי עבר, תוצאה הווה)
> "If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now."

**משמעות:** **מעבר בין זמנים** – תנאי בעבר + תוצאה בהווה.

**מציאות:** לא למדתי רפואה בעבר, ולכן אני לא רופא עכשיו.

---

### טבלה מסכמת

| סוג | תנאי | תוצאה | דוגמה | מציאות |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | Present | Present | If X happens, Y happens | תמיד נכון |
| First | Present | Future (will) | If X happens tomorrow, Y will happen | לא ידועה |
| Second | Past | Would + V | If X happened, Y would happen | ההפך בהווה |
| Third | Past Perfect | Would have + V3 | If X had happened, Y would have happened | ההפך בעבר |
| Mixed | Past Perfect | Would + V | If X had happened, Y would be now | ההפך (עבר→הווה) |

### 💡 פחים נפוצים ב-Conditionals

1. **בלבול בין Second ל-Third:**
   - Second (דמיוני בהווה): "If I won the lottery today, I would be happy."
   - Third (דמיוני בעבר): "If I had won yesterday, I would have been happy."

2. **שכחת היפוך המציאות:**
   - ❌ "If she had studied, she would have passed" → "She passed."
   - ✅ "If she had studied, she would have passed" → "She **did not** study, and **did not** pass."

## 3️⃣ Cause & Effect – סיבה ותוצאה

### מילות סיבה (לפני הסיבה)

| מילה | דוגמה |
|---|---|
| because | She was late **because** there was traffic. |
| since | **Since** it was raining, we stayed home. |
| as | He couldn't come **as** he was sick. |
| due to (+ noun) | The flight was cancelled **due to** the weather. |
| owing to (+ noun) | **Owing to** the storm, schools closed. |
| because of (+ noun) | **Because of** the traffic, I was late. |
| on account of (+ noun) | The game was postponed **on account of** the rain. |

### מילות תוצאה (לפני התוצאה)

| מילה | דוגמה |
|---|---|
| so | It was raining, **so** we stayed home. |
| therefore | The road was closed; **therefore**, we took a different route. |
| thus | He studied hard; **thus**, he passed. |
| consequently | The stock fell; **consequently**, many people sold. |
| as a result | It rained. **As a result**, the picnic was cancelled. |
| hence | The results were clear; **hence**, the project was approved. |

### איך לפרפרז בין סיבה לתוצאה?

**Original (Cause first):** "Because it was raining, we stayed home."
**Paraphrase (Effect first):** "It was raining, so we stayed home."
**Paraphrase (With "due to"):** "We stayed home due to the rain."
**Paraphrase (Active verb):** "The rain caused us to stay home."

**שים לב:** כל הגרסאות זהות במשמעות – רק בסדר המילים ובמילת הקישור.

### 💡 פחים נפוצים

1. **בלבול סיבה עם תוצאה:**
   - ❌ "We stayed home because we went out."
   - ✅ "We stayed home because it was raining."

2. **`due to` חייב להיות עם **שם עצם**:**
   - ❌ "Due to it was raining" (שגוי)
   - ✅ "Due to the rain" (נכון)

3. **`because of` גם עם שם עצם:**
   - ❌ "Because of it was raining"
   - ✅ "Because of the rain"

4. **`because` + משפט מלא:**
   - ✅ "Because it was raining"

## 4️⃣ Concession – ניגוד

### מילות ניגוד

| מילה | שימוש | דוגמה |
|---|---|---|
| although | + משפט מלא | **Although** it was raining, we went out. |
| even though | + משפט מלא | **Even though** he was tired, he kept working. |
| though | + משפט מלא (informal) | She came **though** she was sick. |
| despite | + noun / V-ing | **Despite** the rain, we went out. |
| in spite of | + noun / V-ing | **In spite of** his efforts, he failed. |
| however | משפט חדש | It was raining. **However**, we went out. |
| nevertheless | משפט חדש (formal) | He was tired. **Nevertheless**, he continued. |
| nonetheless | כמו nevertheless | She disagreed, **nonetheless** she voted yes. |
| while | + משפט מלא | **While** he is strict, he is also fair. |
| whereas | השוואה מנוגדת | He is quiet, **whereas** she is loud. |

### טבלת המרות זהות

כל אלה מעבירים את אותה משמעות:

| צורה | דוגמה |
|---|---|
| Although + clause | **Although it was late**, we kept talking. |
| Even though + clause | **Even though it was late**, we kept talking. |
| Despite + noun | **Despite the late hour**, we kept talking. |
| In spite of + noun | **In spite of the late hour**, we kept talking. |
| Despite + V-ing | **Despite being late**, we kept talking. |
| Main clause + however | It was late. **However**, we kept talking. |
| Main clause + nevertheless | It was late. **Nevertheless**, we kept talking. |

### מבני Concessive מתקדמים

#### 1. Concessive Inversion
**"Adjective + as + subject + may/might + be"** = "Although subject may be adjective"

- "**Tired as I was**, I kept working." = "Although I was tired, I kept working."
- "**Strange as it may seem**, he told the truth." = "Although it may seem strange, he told the truth."

#### 2. Much as (concession)
- "**Much as I like her**, I can't work with her." = "Although I like her a lot, I can't work with her."

#### 3. Notwithstanding (פורמלי)
- "**Notwithstanding the difficulties**, they succeeded." = "Despite the difficulties, they succeeded."

### 💡 פחים נפוצים ב-Concession

1. **ערבוב between `Although` ו-`Despite`:**
   - ❌ "Although the rain, we went out." (Although צריך clause)
   - ✅ "Although it was raining, we went out."
   - ✅ "Despite the rain, we went out."

2. **בלבול בין `However` ו-`Although`:**
   - `Although` בתוך משפט אחד
   - `However` בין שני משפטים
   
   דוגמה:
   - ✅ "Although it rained, we went out."
   - ✅ "It rained. However, we went out."
   - ❌ "Although it rained however we went out." (שגיאה כפולה)

## 5️⃣ Comparison – השוואה

### Comparative (יחסי)

#### צורות:
- **Short adjectives (1 syllable):** adjective + -er + than
  - tall → taller than
  - fast → faster than
- **Long adjectives (2+ syllables):** more + adjective + than
  - interesting → more interesting than
  - difficult → more difficult than
- **Irregular:**
  - good → better
  - bad → worse
  - far → further/farther

### Superlative (מוחלט)

#### צורות:
- **Short:** the + adjective + -est
  - tall → the tallest
- **Long:** the most + adjective
  - interesting → the most interesting
- **Irregular:**
  - good → the best
  - bad → the worst

### השוואות שוות (As...as)

- "She is **as tall as** her brother." (גובה זהה)
- "This book is **not as interesting as** the other one." (= less interesting)
- "He works **as hard as** his colleagues." (עבודה ברמת מאמץ זהה)

### השוואות כפולות

- "**The more** you study, **the better** your grades will be." (ככל ש... ככל ש...)
- "**The harder** he works, **the more** tired he feels."

### פרפרזות נפוצות של השוואה

#### 1. Comparative → Reverse Comparative
- "X is bigger than Y." = "Y is smaller than X."
- "She is younger than he is." = "He is older than she is."

#### 2. Superlative → Negation
- "He is **the fastest** runner." = "**No other** runner is as fast as him."
- "This is **the best** movie." = "**No other** movie is as good as this one."

#### 3. Not as...as → Less/More than
- "X is not as tall as Y." = "Y is taller than X." = "X is shorter than Y."

### 💡 פחים נפוצים בהשוואות

1. **היפוך תפקידים:**
   - ❌ "X is older than Y" → "Y is older than X"
   - ✅ "X is older than Y" → "Y is **younger** than X"

2. **Superlative דורש `the`:**
   - ❌ "She is fastest."
   - ✅ "She is **the** fastest."

3. **בלבול בין comparative ל-superlative:**
   - Comparative (שניים): "X is **taller than** Y."
   - Superlative (יותר מ-2): "X is **the tallest** in the group."

## 📚 סיכום: המבנים העיקריים

### טבלת המרות מהירות

| מהמקור | אל הפרפרזה |
|---|---|
| Active | Passive |
| Passive | Active |
| Because of X | Due to X / Owing to X / As a result of X |
| Although X | Despite X / In spite of X / Even though X |
| If + Past Perfect | Reality (what actually happened - opposite) |
| X is taller than Y | Y is shorter than X |
| X is the tallest | No other is as tall as X |
| Direct speech | Reported speech (zam shift) |

### 10 עקרונות זהב לפרפרזות

1. **שמור על כל המידע** – אל תוסיף, אל תגרע.
2. **שמור על הטון** – אם המקור זהיר, הפרפרזה זהירה.
3. **שים לב לזמן הפועל** – אל תשנה אם אין סיבה.
4. **זהה את המבנה המרכזי** – conditional? passive? concession?
5. **בדוק מי עושה מה** – תפקידי הנושא והאובייקט.
6. **זהה סיבה מול ניגוד** – בלבול קלאסי.
7. **ב-Third Conditional חפש את ההפך** – מה שלא קרה.
8. **הכר מילים נרדפות** – because = since = as = due to.
9. **היזהר ממילים קיצוניות** – always, never, all, only.
10. **בחר פשוט על פני מורכב** – אם שתי אפשרויות "נכונות", הפשוטה יותר היא הנכונה.
