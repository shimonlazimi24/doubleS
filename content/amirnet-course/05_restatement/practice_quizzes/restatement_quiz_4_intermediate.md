# Restatement – Practice Quiz #4 (INTERMEDIATE LEVEL)
## מבחן תרגול ניסוח מחדש – מבחן 4 (רמה בינונית)

**מספר שאלות:** 10
**זמן מומלץ:** 15 דקות
**רמת קושי:** בינונית (Intermediate)

---

## Part 1: Questions

### Question 1
**Original:** She promised to call me as soon as she arrived.

(A) She called me before she arrived.
(B) She promised she would call me immediately upon her arrival.
(C) She forgot to call me when she arrived.
(D) She said she would call me the day after she arrived.

---

### Question 2
**Original:** The workers refused to accept the new contract.

(A) The workers happily agreed to the new contract.
(B) The workers did not agree to the new contract.
(C) The workers wrote a new contract themselves.
(D) The workers had no opinion about the new contract.

---

### Question 3
**Original:** Unless the weather improves, the outdoor event will be cancelled.

(A) The outdoor event will take place regardless of the weather.
(B) The outdoor event has already been cancelled.
(C) If the weather does not improve, the outdoor event will not take place.
(D) The weather is expected to improve, so the event will continue.

---

### Question 4
**Original:** Tom is too young to drive a car.

(A) Tom is old enough to drive a car.
(B) Tom drives a car very often.
(C) Tom is so young that he cannot drive a car.
(D) Tom will never be old enough to drive.

---

### Question 5
**Original:** The library has been closed for renovations since last month.

(A) The library closed for renovations one month ago and is still closed.
(B) The library reopened last month after renovations.
(C) The library is scheduled to close next month.
(D) The library has never undergone any renovations.

---

### Question 6
**Original:** The company owes its success to its talented employees.

(A) The company's success is due to its talented employees.
(B) The company's employees are not very talented.
(C) The employees owe money to the company.
(D) The company has not been successful despite its employees.

---

### Question 7
**Original:** She has neither the time nor the money to travel abroad.

(A) She has enough time and money to travel abroad.
(B) She lacks both time and money for foreign travel.
(C) She has the time but not the money to travel.
(D) She has the money but not the time to travel.

---

### Question 8
**Original:** By the time the ambulance arrived, the patient had already recovered.

(A) The patient recovered after the ambulance arrived.
(B) The ambulance arrived before the patient recovered.
(C) The patient was already better when the ambulance got there.
(D) The ambulance did not come to help the patient.

---

### Question 9
**Original:** Despite his young age, he demonstrated remarkable maturity.

(A) Although he was young, he showed impressive maturity.
(B) He was too young to be mature.
(C) His age matched his level of maturity.
(D) He became mature only as he grew older.

---

### Question 10
**Original:** It is likely that the meeting will be postponed.

(A) The meeting has definitely been postponed.
(B) The meeting will probably be delayed to a later date.
(C) The meeting will certainly happen on time.
(D) The meeting has been cancelled permanently.

---

## Part 2: Answers and Explanations

### ✅ Q1 – Correct Answer: **(B)**

**משפט מקורי:** היא הבטיחה לקרוא לי ברגע שהיא תגיע.

`as soon as she arrived` = `immediately upon her arrival`. שני הביטויים זהים במשמעות.

- ❌ (A) "called me before" - סותר את "as soon as arrived" (בהגעה עצמה, לא לפני).
- ✅ (B) "immediately upon her arrival" - פרפרזה מדויקת של "as soon as she arrived".
- ❌ (C) "forgot to call" - סותר "she promised".
- ❌ (D) "the day after" - סותר "as soon as" (מיד, לא יום אחרי).

**💡 טיפ:** `as soon as` = `immediately when/upon` = `the moment [that]`.

---

### ✅ Q2 – Correct Answer: **(B)**

**משפט מקורי:** העובדים סירבו לקבל את החוזה החדש.

`refused to accept` = `did not agree to`. ביטויים נרדפים.

- ❌ (A) "happily agreed" - הפוך לחלוטין.
- ✅ (B) "did not agree to" - פרפרזה מדויקת של "refused to accept".
- ❌ (C) "wrote a new contract themselves" - הוספת מידע לא קיים.
- ❌ (D) "had no opinion" - סותר "refused" (שמבטא עמדה ברורה).

---

### ✅ Q3 – Correct Answer: **(C)**

**משפט מקורי:** אם מזג האוויר לא ישתפר, האירוע החיצוני יבוטל.

`Unless X` = `If not X`. מבנה תנאי שלילי.

- ❌ (A) "regardless of the weather" - סותר את התנאי.
- ❌ (B) "already been cancelled" - עבר, לא עתיד.
- ✅ (C) "If the weather does not improve... will not take place" - פרפרזה של "unless X, Y" = "If not X, not Y".
- ❌ (D) "expected to improve... will continue" - ההפך של התנאי.

**💡 טיפ:** `Unless X happens, Y will be cancelled` = `If X does not happen, Y will be cancelled`. **אותו רעיון!**

---

### ✅ Q4 – Correct Answer: **(C)**

**משפט מקורי:** טום צעיר מדי כדי לנהוג.

`too + adjective + to V` = `so + adjective + that [subject] + cannot V`.

- ❌ (A) "old enough" - הפוך.
- ❌ (B) "drives very often" - סותר "cannot drive".
- ✅ (C) "so young that he cannot drive" - פרפרזה מדויקת של "too young to drive".
- ❌ (D) "never be old enough" - חזק מדי; המשפט המקורי לא שולל יכולת עתידית.

**💡 טיפ:** `too X to V` = `so X that cannot V`. מבנה חשוב.

---

### ✅ Q5 – Correct Answer: **(A)**

**משפט מקורי:** הספרייה סגורה לשיפוצים מאז החודש שעבר.

`has been closed since last month` = Present Perfect Continuous במהותו - פעולה שהתחילה בעבר וממשיכה להווה.

- ✅ (A) "closed one month ago and is still closed" - פרפרזה מדויקת של Present Perfect.
- ❌ (B) "reopened last month" - הפוך.
- ❌ (C) "scheduled to close next month" - עתיד, לא הווה.
- ❌ (D) "never undergone any renovations" - סותר.

**💡 טיפ:** `has been + adjective + since X` = "מאז X ועד היום".

---

### ✅ Q6 – Correct Answer: **(A)**

**משפט מקורי:** החברה חייבת את הצלחתה לעובדים המוכשרים שלה.

`owes X to Y` = `X is due to Y`. מבנה של ייחוס.

- ✅ (A) "success is due to talented employees" - פרפרזה מדויקת של "owes success to".
- ❌ (B) "not very talented" - הפוך.
- ❌ (C) "owe money to the company" - בלבול מילה "owe" (המילה יכולה להיות חוב כספי, אבל כאן זה שייכות להצלחה).
- ❌ (D) "has not been successful" - סותר.

**💡 טיפ:** `X owes Y to Z` = `Z is the reason for Y`. ביטוי נפוץ לייחוס הצלחה.

---

### ✅ Q7 – Correct Answer: **(B)**

**משפט מקורי:** אין לה לא זמן ולא כסף לנסוע לחוץ לארץ.

`neither X nor Y` = `lacks both X and Y`.

- ❌ (A) "has enough" - הפוך.
- ✅ (B) "lacks both time and money" - פרפרזה מדויקת של "neither... nor".
- ❌ (C) "time but not money" - שגוי; המקור אומר אין **שניהם**.
- ❌ (D) "money but not time" - שגוי אותה סיבה.

---

### ✅ Q8 – Correct Answer: **(C)**

**משפט מקורי:** עד שהאמבולנס הגיע, החולה כבר התאושש.

`By the time X, Y had already V3` = כשקרה X, Y כבר היה גמור. מבנה של Past Perfect.

- ❌ (A) "recovered after the ambulance arrived" - היפוך הסדר הכרונולוגי.
- ❌ (B) "ambulance arrived before the patient recovered" - היפוך.
- ✅ (C) "patient was already better when the ambulance got there" - תופס את הסדר הנכון.
- ❌ (D) "did not come to help" - לא נאמר.

**💡 טיפ:** `By the time X happened, Y had already happened` = "כש-X קרה, Y כבר היה מאחורינו".

---

### ✅ Q9 – Correct Answer: **(A)**

**משפט מקורי:** למרות גילו הצעיר, הוא הפגין בגרות יוצאת דופן.

`Despite X, Y` = `Although X, Y`. שני ביטויי ניגוד זהים.

- ✅ (A) "Although he was young, he showed impressive maturity" - פרפרזה מדויקת.
- ❌ (B) "too young to be mature" - סותר; המקור אומר שהוא **כן** הפגין בגרות.
- ❌ (C) "age matched his maturity" - סותר את הניגוד.
- ❌ (D) "only as he grew older" - סותר "young age".

---

### ✅ Q10 – Correct Answer: **(B)**

**משפט מקורי:** יש סבירות שהפגישה תידחה.

`It is likely that X` = `X will probably happen`. רמת וודאות בינונית.

- ❌ (A) "definitely been postponed" - חזק מדי; "likely" לא = "definitely".
- ✅ (B) "probably be delayed" - פרפרזה מדויקת של "likely".
- ❌ (C) "certainly happen on time" - הפוך.
- ❌ (D) "cancelled permanently" - "postponed" ≠ "cancelled".

**💡 טיפ:** שימו לב לרמות הוודאות:
- `certain/definitely` = ודאי (100%)
- `likely/probably` = סביר (70-80%)
- `possible/may/might` = אפשרי (30-50%)
- `unlikely` = לא סביר
