## Part 1: Questions

### Question 1 🟢
**Original:** The coffee is too hot to drink.

(A) The coffee is at the perfect temperature for drinking.
(B) The coffee is so hot that I cannot drink it.
(C) The coffee is cold, not hot.
(D) I drink the coffee even though it is hot.

---

### Question 2 🟢
**Original:** Mary is happy because she passed the test.

(A) Mary failed the test and is sad about it.
(B) Mary did not take the test at all.
(C) Mary is happy, and the reason is that she passed the test.
(D) Mary is sad despite passing the test.

---

### Question 3 🟢
**Original:** I have never been to London.

(A) I went to London last year.
(B) I have been to London many times.
(C) I will go to London tomorrow for the first time.
(D) I have not visited London at any time in my life.

---

### Question 4 🟢
**Original:** I prefer tea to coffee.

(A) I like coffee more than tea.
(B) I do not like either tea or coffee.
(C) I like tea more than I like coffee.

---

### Question 5 🟡
**Original:** She is used to working late at the office.

(A) She is working late for the first time.

(C) She cannot work late under any conditions.
(D) She used to work late, but no longer does.

### Question 6 🟡
**Original:** The book was borrowed by my brother.

(A) My brother borrowed the book.

### Question 7 🟡
**Original:** He runs faster than anyone else on the team.

(A) Some team members run faster than he does.
(B) He is the fastest runner on the team.
(C) The whole team runs at the same speed.
(D) He does not run at all.

### Question 8 🟡
**Original:** Despite being tired, she continued working.

(A) She stopped working because she was tired.
(B) She was not tired and kept working.

(D) She rested before continuing to work.

### Question 9 🟡

### Question 10 🔴

### Question 14 🔴

### Question 15 🔴

## Part 2: Answers and Explanations

### ✅ Q1 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟢
`too X to V` = `so X that can't V`. מבנה בסיסי שחשוב לדעת.
"too hot to drink" = "so hot I cannot drink it".

### ✅ Q2 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🟢
`X because Y` = `X, and the reason is Y`. פרפרזה ישירה של סיבה.

### ✅ Q3 – Correct Answer: **(D)** 🟢
`have never been to X` = `have not visited X at any time`.
(A), (B), (C) כולן סותרות את "never".

### ✅ Q4 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🟢
`prefer X to Y` = `like X more than Y`. מבנה של העדפה.

### ✅ Q5 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟡
**פח נפוץ!** יש הבדל בין:
- `is used to V-ing` = רגיל ל-, עושה באופן קבוע
- `used to V` = היה עושה (הרגל בעבר שהסתיים)

המשפט הוא "is used to working" = עושה באופן קבוע = (B).
(D) משתמשת ב-"used to" = הרגל בעבר. שגוי!

**💡 טיפ:** זהו את ה-"is" לפני "used to" - זה משנה הכל.

### ✅ Q6 – Correct Answer: **(A)** 🟡
Passive → Active: `The X was V-ed by Y` = `Y V-ed the X`.
"Book was borrowed by brother" = "Brother borrowed the book".

### ✅ Q7 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟡
`X runs faster than anyone else on Y` = `X is the fastest runner on Y`.
Superlative דרך השוואה.

### ✅ Q8 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🟡
`Despite + V-ing` = `Although + subject + be + adjective`.
"Despite being tired" = "Although she was tired". שני מבנים נרדפים של ניגוד.

### ✅ Q9 – Correct Answer: **(A)** 🟡
Relative clause: "The movie, **which was based on a true story**, became successful."
= "A movie based on a true story became successful."
(B) היפוך שגוי של הקשר.

### ✅ Q10 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🔴
**Inversion:** `Rarely have I seen such X` = `X like this is something I have seldom seen`.
Rarely = seldom (נדיר).
(A) הפוך לחלוטין.

**💡 טיפ:** לאחר `Rarely`, `Seldom`, `Hardly` בתחילת משפט - inversion.

### ✅ Q11 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🔴
**Third Conditional with Inversion:**
"Had the evidence been examined more carefully" = "If the evidence had been examined more carefully".
משמעות: במציאות - הראיות **לא** נבדקו מספיק טוב, ולכן הייתה טעות.

(A) סותר - הטעות כן קרתה.
(C) לא נכון - הטעות הייתה מרשלנות, לא מכוונת.

### ✅ Q12 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🔴
`Under no circumstances should X V` = `X must never V, regardless of the situation`.
"Under no circumstances" = בשום אופן = **אף פעם**, בכל מצב.

### ✅ Q13 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🔴
**Nominalization unpacked:**
- "The implementation of the new system" → "When the new system was introduced"
- "was met with considerable resistance from employees" → "many employees strongly opposed it"

(A) ההפך. (C) ההפך. (D) ההפך.

### ✅ Q14 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🔴
**Inversion after "Only after":** `Only after X did Y` = `Y happened only after X`.
"Only after investigation was complete did truth emerge" = "Truth came out only after investigation finished".

### ✅ Q15 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🔴
`Not only did X fail, but it also damaged Y` = `X did not succeed + X harmed Y`.
שלושה רעיונות: (1) לא הפחית עלויות, (2) פגע במורל, (3) שתי תוצאות שליליות.

(C) תופס את שלושתם: "neither reduced... nor helped... actually harmed both".

## 📋 Part 4: פחים חשובים שעסקנו בהם

1. **"is used to" vs "used to"** - שאלה 5. זהו את ה-"is"!
2. **Inversion** - שאלות 10, 11, 12, 14, 15. זיהוי מיידי של "Rarely", "Had", "Under no", "Only after", "Not only".
3. **Third Conditional** - שאלה 11. "Had X been, Y would have been" = במציאות X לא קרה.
4. **Nominalization** - שאלה 13. "The implementation of X" → "When X was implemented".
5. **Relative Clauses** - שאלה 9. "The Y, which was Z, did W" → "A Y based on Z did W".
