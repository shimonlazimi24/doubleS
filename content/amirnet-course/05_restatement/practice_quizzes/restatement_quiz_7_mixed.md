## Part 1: Questions

### Question 1 🟢
**Original:** The store will open at 9 AM tomorrow.

(A) The store will close at 9 AM tomorrow.
(B) The store will open at 9 o'clock in the morning tomorrow.
(C) The store opens at 9 AM every day.
(D) The store was open yesterday at 9 AM.

## Part 2: Answers and Explanations

### ✅ Q1 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟢
"9 AM" = "9 o'clock in the morning". פרפרזה ישירה של הזמן.

### ✅ Q2 – Correct Answer: **(A)** 🟢
`be good at V-ing` = `V well`. "She is good at playing" = "She plays well".

### ✅ Q3 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🟢
`don't like X` = `X is not something I enjoy`. (D) שגויה כי "never tasted" חזקה מדי.

### ✅ Q4 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟡
`give up V-ing` = `stop trying to V`. Phrasal verb synonym.

### ✅ Q5 – Correct Answer: **(A)** 🟡
**Reported Speech → Direct Speech:**
- "would call me the next day" (reported) → "I will call you tomorrow" (direct)
- `would` ← `will` | `the next day` ← `tomorrow`

**💡 טיפ:** המרת דיבור עקיף לישיר: הזזת זמן אחורה, שינוי זמן יחסי.

### ✅ Q6 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟡
`should + might` = המלצה + אפשרות = `advisable` + `possibility`. 
(A) "must" ו-"is raining" חזקים מדי.
(C) "will definitely" סותר "might".

### ✅ Q7 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟡
`If I were you, I would X` = `In my opinion, you should X`. 
Second Conditional עם "If I were you" = עצה.

### ✅ Q8 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟢
**Passive → Active:**
- `X was written by Y` = `Y wrote X`.

### ✅ Q9 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🔴
**Cleft sentence:** `It was X that Y` = `X was the one that Y`.
**💡 טיפ:** מבנה cleft מדגיש את X; כלומר, מכל ההצעות, **דווקא** של ג'ון שינתה הכל.

### ✅ Q10 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🔴
`Not only X, but also Y` = `X, and additionally Y`. שני דברים קרו.
(A) ו-(C) סותרים חלק אחד; (D) סותר את שניהם.

### ✅ Q11 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🔴
**Subjunctive:** `It is crucial that X finish` (bare infinitive) = `X absolutely must complete`.
`crucial` = חיוני/קריטי = `absolutely must`.

### ✅ Q12 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🟡
**Nominalization unpacked:**
- "The increase in prices" (שם פעולה) → "Higher prices" (תואר)
- "has led to decreased sales" (שם פעולה) → "has resulted in lower sales"
**💡 טיפ:** פירוק שמות פעולה הוא טכניקה מרכזית במבחן.

### ✅ Q13 – Correct Answer: **(A)** 🔴
`more X than any of Y` = `None of Y is as X as...` 
**💡 טיפ:** דרך ליצור superlative דרך שלילה. קלאסי במבחנים.

### ✅ Q14 – Correct Answer: **(B)** 🔴
`Owing to X, Y was delayed` = `X caused Y to be delayed`.
`Owing to` = `Because of` = `Due to`. מילה פורמלית לסיבה.

### ✅ Q15 – Correct Answer: **(C)** 🔴
`Rejected unanimously by the committee` = `Every committee member voted against`.
`Unanimously` = פה אחד, כלומר **כולם** הסכימו לדחות.
