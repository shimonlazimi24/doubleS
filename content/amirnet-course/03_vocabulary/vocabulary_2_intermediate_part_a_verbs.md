# 🏃 קטגוריה 1: פעלי פעולה מתקדמים (30 מילים)

### 501. **achieve** (v.)
- **Definition:** to successfully reach a goal
- **תרגום:** להשיג
- **Example 1:** She worked hard to **achieve** her dream.
- **Example 2:** The team **achieved** great results.
- **Synonyms:** accomplish, attain
- **Antonyms:** fail, miss
- **💡 Memory tip:** Collocation: `achieve a goal/success/victory`.

### 502. **accomplish** (v.)
- **Definition:** to complete successfully
- **תרגום:** להשיג, להשלים
- **Example 1:** We **accomplished** all our tasks today.
- **Example 2:** He **accomplished** a lot in his career.
- **Synonyms:** achieve, complete
- **Antonyms:** fail
- **💡 Memory tip:** פורמלי יותר מ-`achieve`.

### 503. **acquire** (v.)
- **Definition:** to gain or obtain
- **תרגום:** לרכוש, להשיג
- **Example 1:** The company **acquired** a new office.
- **Example 2:** Children **acquire** language naturally.
- **Synonyms:** obtain, gain
- **Antonyms:** lose
- **💡 Memory tip:** Collocation: `acquire skills/knowledge`.

### 504. **obtain** (v.)
- **Definition:** to get something, often with effort
- **תרגום:** להשיג, לקבל
- **Example 1:** You need to **obtain** permission first.
- **Example 2:** She **obtained** a degree in law.
- **Synonyms:** acquire, get
- **Antonyms:** lose
- **💡 Memory tip:** פורמלי יותר מ-`get`.

### 505. **gain** (v.)
- **Definition:** to get something useful
- **תרגום:** לרכוש, להרוויח
- **Example 1:** Exercise helps you **gain** strength.
- **Example 2:** He **gained** valuable experience.
- **Synonyms:** acquire, obtain
- **Antonyms:** lose
- **💡 Memory tip:** `gain weight/experience/knowledge`.

### 506. **provide** (v.)
- **Definition:** to give something needed
- **תרגום:** לספק, לתת
- **Example 1:** The school **provides** free lunch.
- **Example 2:** Can you **provide** more information?
- **Synonyms:** supply, give, offer
- **Antonyms:** deny, withhold
- **💡 Memory tip:** `provide X for/with Y`.

### 507. **supply** (v.)

- **תרגום:** לספק

- **Example 2:** They **supply** us with everything we need.
- **Synonyms:** provide, give
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: "supply and demand".

### 508. **deliver** (v.)

- **תרגום:** לספק, להעביר
- **Example 1:** We **deliver** pizza to your door.
- **Example 2:** She **delivered** a great speech.
- **Synonyms:** bring, transport, give
- **💡 Memory tip:** `deliver a speech/presentation/package`.

### 509. **transport** (v.)

- **תרגום:** להעביר, להוביל
- **Example 1:** Trains **transport** passengers daily.
- **Example 2:** The ship **transports** goods overseas.
- **Synonyms:** carry, move, deliver
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: `public transport`.

### 510. **produce** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make or create
- **תרגום:** לייצר, לייצר
- **Example 1:** This factory **produces** cars.
- **Example 2:** The plant **produces** fruit every year.
- **Synonyms:** make, create, manufacture
- **Antonyms:** destroy
- **💡 Memory tip:** הגייה: פועל /prəˈduːs/, שם עצם /ˈprɒdjuːs/.

### 511. **create** (v.)

- **Synonyms:** make, produce, build
- **Antonyms:** destroy
- **💡 Memory tip:** `create` = יצירה מחדש; `produce` = ייצור כמותי.

### 512. **design** (v.)

- **Synonyms:** deter, dishearten

### 518. **recommend** (v.)

- **תרגום:** להמליץ
- **Example 1:** I **recommend** this book.
- **Example 2:** The doctor **recommended** rest.
- **Synonyms:** suggest, advise
- **💡 Memory tip:** `recommend + V-ing` או `recommend that + subjunctive`.

### 519. **suggest** (v.)
- **Definition:** to propose an idea
- **תרגום:** להציע, לרמוז
- **Example 1:** I **suggest** we leave early.
- **Example 2:** The evidence **suggests** he's innocent.
- **Synonyms:** propose, recommend, imply

### 520. **propose** (v.)
- **Definition:** to put forward an idea
- **תרגום:** להציע
- **Example 1:** She **proposed** a new plan.
- **Example 2:** He **proposed** marriage to her.
- **Synonyms:** suggest, offer
- **💡 Memory tip:** יותר פורמלי מ-`suggest`. גם: להציע נישואין.

### 521. **offer** (v.)
- **Definition:** to present for acceptance
- **תרגום:** להציע
- **Example 1:** They **offered** me a job.
- **Example 2:** Can I **offer** you some coffee?
- **Synonyms:** provide, present
- **Antonyms:** refuse
- **💡 Memory tip:** `offer X to Y` / `offer to V`.

### 522. **refuse** (v.)
- **Definition:** to say no to something
- **תרגום:** לסרב
- **Example 1:** He **refused** to help me.
- **Example 2:** She **refused** the invitation.
- **Synonyms:** decline, reject
- **Antonyms:** accept, agree
- **💡 Memory tip:** `refuse to V` = לסרב לעשות.

### 523. **reject** (v.)
- **Definition:** to not accept
- **תרגום:** לדחות
- **Example 1:** The committee **rejected** the proposal.
- **Example 2:** She **rejected** the offer.
- **Synonyms:** refuse, decline, turn down
- **Antonyms:** accept, approve
- **💡 Memory tip:** פורמלי יותר מ-`refuse`; בהקשר החלטות.

### 524. **approve** (v.)
- **Definition:** to accept as good; to give permission
- **תרגום:** לאשר
- **Example 1:** The boss **approved** the project.
- **Example 2:** I don't **approve** of smoking.
- **Synonyms:** accept, sanction
- **Antonyms:** reject, disapprove
- **💡 Memory tip:** `approve of X` = לאשר/לחזק X.

### 525. **request** (v.)
- **Definition:** to ask politely for
- **תרגום:** לבקש
- **Example 1:** She **requested** a glass of water.
- **Example 2:** I **request** your attention.
- **Synonyms:** ask, seek
- **💡 Memory tip:** פורמלי יותר מ-`ask`.

### 526. **demand** (v.)
- **Definition:** to ask forcefully
- **תרגום:** לדרוש
- **Example 1:** Workers **demanded** higher pay.
- **Example 2:** The situation **demands** attention.
- **Synonyms:** require, insist on
- **💡 Memory tip:** חזק יותר מ-`request` או `ask`.

### 527. **require** (v.)
- **Definition:** to need
- **תרגום:** לדרוש, להזדקק
- **Example 1:** This job **requires** experience.
- **Example 2:** Plants **require** water to grow.
- **Synonyms:** need, demand
- **💡 Memory tip:** פורמלי יותר מ-`need`.

### 528. **prevent** (v.)
- **Definition:** to stop something from happening
- **תרגום:** למנוע
- **Example 1:** Exercise **prevents** disease.
- **Example 2:** Rain **prevented** the game.
- **Synonyms:** stop, block, avoid
- **Antonyms:** allow, enable
- **💡 Memory tip:** `prevent someone from V-ing`.

### 529. **cause** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make something happen
- **תרגום:** לגרום
- **Example 1:** Smoking **causes** cancer.
- **Example 2:** The storm **caused** damage.
- **Synonyms:** produce, result in
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: "cause and effect".

### 530. **affect** (v.)
- **Definition:** to have an effect on
- **תרגום:** להשפיע על
- **Example 1:** The news **affected** everyone.
- **Example 2:** Stress **affects** your health.
- **Synonyms:** influence, impact
- **💡 Memory tip:** **affect** (פועל) ≠ **effect** (שם עצם)!

- **Example 1:** The box **contains** chocolates.
- **Example 2:** The book **contains** many stories.
- **Synonyms:** hold, include
- **💡 Memory tip:** חפץ + מה שבתוכו.

### 638. **depend** (v.)
- **Definition:** to rely on
- **תרגום:** להיות תלוי
- **Example 1:** It **depends** on the weather.
- **Example 2:** I **depend** on my car for work.
- **Synonyms:** rely on, count on
- **💡 Memory tip:** `depend on X` - תמיד עם "on"!

### 639. **rely** (v.)
- **Definition:** to depend on
- **תרגום:** לסמוך על
- **Example 1:** You can **rely** on me.
- **Example 2:** We **rely** on technology.
- **Synonyms:** depend on, trust
- **💡 Memory tip:** `rely on X`.

### 640. **tend** (v.)
- **Definition:** to have a usual way; to care for
- **תרגום:** לנטות; לטפל ב-
- **Example 1:** I **tend** to arrive early.
- **Example 2:** She **tends** the garden.
- **Synonyms:** be inclined to; care for
- **💡 Memory tip:** `tend to V` = לנטות לעשות.

### 641. **apply** (v.)
- **Definition:** to put to use; to request
- **תרגום:** ליישם; להגיש מועמדות
- **Example 1:** **Apply** this cream to your skin.
- **Example 2:** I **applied** for the job.
- **Synonyms:** use, request
- **💡 Memory tip:** שני מובנים נפוצים.

### 642. **permit** (v.)
- **Definition:** to allow
- **תרגום:** להתיר, לאפשר
- **Example 1:** We don't **permit** smoking here.
- **Example 2:** Weather **permitting**, we'll go.
- **Synonyms:** allow, let
- **Antonyms:** forbid, prohibit
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: `a permit` (אישור).

### 643. **prohibit** (v.)
- **Definition:** to forbid
- **תרגום:** לאסור
- **Example 1:** Smoking is **prohibited**.
- **Example 2:** The law **prohibits** such actions.
- **Synonyms:** forbid, ban
- **Antonyms:** allow, permit
- **💡 Memory tip:** פורמלי מאוד בהקשר חוקי.

### 644. **forbid** (v.)
- **Definition:** to order not to do
- **תרגום:** לאסור
- **Example 1:** My parents **forbade** me to go.
- **Example 2:** The rules **forbid** running.
- **Synonyms:** prohibit, ban
- **Antonyms:** allow, permit
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: forbid/forbids/forbade/forbidden.

### 645. **suspect** (v.)
- **Definition:** to believe something bad is true
- **תרגום:** לחשוד
- **Example 1:** I **suspect** he's lying.
- **Example 2:** The police **suspect** foul play.
- **Synonyms:** doubt, distrust
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: `a suspect` (חשוד).

### 646. **accuse** (v.)
- **Definition:** to say someone did wrong
- **תרגום:** להאשים
- **Example 1:** Don't **accuse** me without proof.
- **Example 2:** He was **accused** of theft.
- **Synonyms:** blame, charge
- **Antonyms:** defend
- **💡 Memory tip:** `accuse someone of X` = להאשים מישהו ב-X.

### 647. **blame** (v.)
- **Definition:** to say someone is responsible for wrong
- **תרגום:** להאשים
- **Example 1:** Don't **blame** me for your mistakes.
- **Example 2:** She **blames** herself.
- **Synonyms:** accuse, fault
- **Antonyms:** praise
- **💡 Memory tip:** `blame X for Y` / `blame X on Y`.

### 648. **praise** (v.)
- **Definition:** to express approval
- **תרגום:** לשבח
- **Example 1:** The teacher **praised** her work.
- **Example 2:** He was **praised** for his bravery.
- **Synonyms:** commend, compliment
- **Antonyms:** criticize, blame
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: "high praise".

### 649. **criticize** (v.)
- **Definition:** to express disapproval
- **תרגום:** לבקר
- **Example 1:** Don't **criticize** without evidence.
- **Example 2:** The report was widely **criticized**.
- **Synonyms:** blame, judge, fault
- **Antonyms:** praise
- **💡 Memory tip:** `critic` = מבקר.

### 650. **respect** (v.)
- **Definition:** to hold in high regard
- **תרגום:** לכבד
- **Example 1:** **Respect** your elders.
- **Example 2:** I **respect** your opinion.
- **Synonyms:** honor, admire
- **Antonyms:** disrespect, disdain
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם.
