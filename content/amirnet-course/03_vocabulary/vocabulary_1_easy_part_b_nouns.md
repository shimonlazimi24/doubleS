# 👥 קטגוריה 1: אנשים ויחסי משפחה (30 מילים)

### 151. **person** (n.)
- **Definition:** a human being
- **תרגום:** אדם, בן אדם
- **Example 1:** He is a nice **person**.
- **Example 2:** Each **person** has unique talents.
- **Synonyms:** individual, human
- **💡 Memory tip:** Plural: **people** (לא "persons" באנגלית יומיומית).

### 152. **people** (n.)
- **Definition:** human beings in general (plural)
- **תרגום:** אנשים
- **Example 1:** Many **people** came to the concert.
- **Example 2:** **People** are different from each other.
- **Synonyms:** humans, individuals
- **💡 Memory tip:** תמיד ברבים - "people are" (לא "people is").

### 153. **man** (n.)
- **Definition:** an adult male human
- **תרגום:** גבר, איש
- **Example 1:** The **man** at the door is my neighbor.
- **Example 2:** A young **man** helped me.
- **Synonyms:** male, gentleman
- **Antonyms:** woman
- **💡 Memory tip:** Plural: **men** (לא "mans").

### 177. **king** (n.)

# 🏠 קטגוריה 2: מקומות ומיקומים (30 מילים)

### 181. **place** (n.)
- **Definition:** a location or area
- **תרגום:** מקום
- **Example 1:** This is a nice **place** to relax.
- **Example 2:** I've been to many **places** in Europe.
- **Synonyms:** location, spot, area
- **💡 Memory tip:** כללי מאוד - שימוש רב-תכליתי.

### 182. **home** (n.)
- **Definition:** the place where you live
- **תרגום:** בית (המקום, הרגש)
- **Example 1:** I'm going **home**.
- **Example 2:** There's no place like **home**.
- **Synonyms:** house, residence
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **home** = בית כרגש/מושג; **house** = בית כמבנה פיזי.

### 183. **house** (n.)
- **Definition:** a building where people live
- **תרגום:** בית (מבנה)
- **Example 1:** They bought a new **house**.
- **Example 2:** The **house** has three bedrooms.
- **Synonyms:** building, residence
- **💡 Memory tip:** Plural: **houses** (הגייה: /ˈhaʊzɪz/).

### 184. **apartment** (n.) / **flat** (British)
- **Definition:** a home in a large building
- **תרגום:** דירה
- **Example 1:** I live in a small **apartment**.
- **Example 2:** My **apartment** is on the third floor.
- **Synonyms:** flat (British)
- **💡 Memory tip:** אמריקאי: apartment. בריטי: flat.

### 185. **room** (n.)
- **Definition:** a part of a building
- **תרגום:** חדר; מקום
- **Example 1:** My **room** is upstairs.
- **Example 2:** There's no **room** for us all in the car.
- **Synonyms:** space, chamber
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם "space" - "is there room for me?".

### 186. **kitchen** (n.)
- **Definition:** the room where you cook
- **תרגום:** מטבח
- **Example 1:** She's in the **kitchen**.
- **Example 2:** Our **kitchen** is small but modern.
- **Synonyms:** (none)
- **💡 Memory tip:** "kitchen" = place where you "catch" food to cook (עזר לזכור).

### 187. **bedroom** (n.)
- **Definition:** a room for sleeping
- **תרגום:** חדר שינה
- **Example 1:** The **bedroom** is painted blue.
- **Example 2:** I have my own **bedroom**.
- **Synonyms:** (none)
- **💡 Memory tip:** bed + room = bedroom.

### 188. **bathroom** (n.)
- **Definition:** a room with a toilet/shower
- **תרגום:** חדר אמבטיה, שירותים
- **Example 1:** Where's the **bathroom**?
- **Example 2:** The **bathroom** needs cleaning.
- **Synonyms:** washroom, restroom (American)
- **💡 Memory tip:** bath + room = bathroom.

### 189. **garden** (n.)
- **Definition:** an area of land with plants
- **תרגום:** גינה
- **Example 1:** We have a small **garden** behind the house.
- **Example 2:** She's working in the **garden**.
- **Synonyms:** yard (American)
- **💡 Memory tip:** "Kindergarten" = גן ילדים (מגרמנית).

### 190. **city** (n.)
- **Definition:** a large town
- **תרגום:** עיר
- **Example 1:** Tel Aviv is a big **city**.
- **Example 2:** She loves living in the **city**.
- **Synonyms:** metropolis, urban area
- **Antonyms:** village, country
- **💡 Memory tip:** Plural: **cities**.

### 191. **town** (n.)
- **Definition:** a small to medium-sized city
- **תרגום:** עיירה
- **Example 1:** It's a quiet **town**.
- **Example 2:** The **town** has only one supermarket.
- **Synonyms:** municipality
- **Antonyms:** village (smaller), city (larger)
- **💡 Memory tip:** קטן יותר מ-city, גדול יותר מ-village.

### 192. **village** (n.)

### 193. **country** (n.)

- **תרגום:** מדינה; אזור כפרי

- **Antonyms:** (none)
- **💡 Memory tip:** שני מובנים! "my country" = המדינה שלי; "the country" = האזור הכפרי.

### 194. **world** (n.)

# 🕐 קטגוריה 3: זמן ותאריכים (30 מילים)

- **Definition:** hours, minutes, seconds; a period
- **תרגום:** זמן
- **Example 1:** What **time** is it?
- **Example 2:** I don't have enough **time**.
- **Synonyms:** period, moment
- **💡 Memory tip:** המילה הנפוצה בעולם! גם פועל: "time something".

### 212. **day** (n.)
- **Definition:** a 24-hour period
- **תרגום:** יום
- **Example 1:** Have a nice **day**!
- **Example 2:** I work eight hours a **day**.
- **Synonyms:** 24 hours
- **Antonyms:** night
- **💡 Memory tip:** Plural: **days**.

### 213. **night** (n.)

- **Example 1:** I sleep 8 hours a **night**.
- **Example 2:** The stars shine at **night**.
- **Synonyms:** evening (late)
- **Antonyms:** day
- **💡 Memory tip:** `at night` (לא "in the night" ברוב המקרים).

### 214. **morning** (n.)

- **Example 1:** Good **morning**!
- **Example 2:** I exercise in the **morning**.
- **Synonyms:** (none)

### 215. **afternoon** (n.)

- **Example 1:** I have a meeting this **afternoon**.
- **Example 2:** The children come home in the **afternoon**.
- **Synonyms:** (none)
- **💡 Memory tip:** after + noon (אחרי צהריים).

### 216. **evening** (n.)
- **Definition:** the time between afternoon and night
- **תרגום:** ערב
- **Example 1:** We had dinner in the **evening**.
- **Example 2:** Good **evening**, sir.
- **Synonyms:** (none)
- **Antonyms:** morning

### 217. **week** (n.)

- **Example 1:** I'll see you next **week**.
- **Example 2:** We spent a **week** in Paris.

## 🧠 כללי זהב לשמות עצם:

### 1. **Countable vs Uncountable**
- **Countable:** book/books, apple/apples, chair/chairs
- **Uncountable** (רק יחיד): water, information, money, news, advice, bread

### 2. **Plural זהיר**
- `child → children` (לא "childs")
- `man → men`, `woman → women`
- `mouse → mice`, `foot → feet`
- `fish → fish` (ללא שינוי!)

### 3. **מילים שתמיד ברבים**
- `clothes`, `scissors`, `pants`, `trousers`, `glasses`

### 4. **Articles (a/an/the)**
- `a` לפני עיצור: a book, a car
- `an` לפני תנועה: an apple, an hour (h silent!)
- `the` לספציפי: the sun, the book (I just mentioned)
