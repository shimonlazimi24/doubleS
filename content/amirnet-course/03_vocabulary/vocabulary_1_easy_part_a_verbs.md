# 📘 חלק א': 150 פעלים (Verbs)

> **טיפ:** פעלים הם הלב של השפה. שליטה ב-150 פעלים הבסיסיים = יכולת להביע כמעט כל רעיון.

## 🟢 Section A1: פעלי יומיום בסיסיים (1-30)

### 1. **be** (v.)
- **Definition:** to exist; to have a particular quality or state
- **תרגום:** להיות
- **Example 1:** I **am** a student at the university.
- **Example 2:** The book **is** on the table.
- **Synonyms:** exist, remain
- **Antonyms:** (none - unique verb)
- **💡 Memory tip:** הפועל הכי בסיסי באנגלית. Forms: am/is/are/was/were/been.

### 2. **have** (v.)
- **Definition:** to possess or own; to experience
- **תרגום:** להיות ל-, להחזיק
- **Example 1:** She **has** two brothers.
- **Example 2:** We **have** a meeting at 3 PM.
- **Synonyms:** possess, own
- **Antonyms:** lack
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: have/has/had. משמש גם לזמני Perfect: "I have eaten".

### 3. **do** (v.)
- **Definition:** to perform an action; to complete
- **תרגום:** לעשות
- **Example 1:** What **do** you do for work?
- **Example 2:** She **did** her homework quickly.
- **Synonyms:** perform, carry out
- **Antonyms:** undo
- **💡 Memory tip:** עוזר הפועל הכי נפוץ לשאלות בהווה: "Do you like...?"

### 4. **go** (v.)
- **Definition:** to move from one place to another
- **תרגום:** ללכת, להגיע ל-
- **Example 1:** I **go** to school every day.
- **Example 2:** They **went** to Paris last summer.
- **Synonyms:** travel, move, head
- **Antonyms:** stay, remain, come
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: go/goes/went/gone. Past = "went" (לא "goed").

### 5. **come** (v.)
- **Definition:** to move toward a place; to arrive
- **תרגום:** לבוא, להגיע
- **Example 1:** Please **come** to my party on Friday.
- **Example 2:** She **came** home late last night.
- **Synonyms:** arrive, approach
- **Antonyms:** go, leave
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: come/comes/came/come. "Come" נשאר גם בעבר השלישי.

### 6. **see** (v.)
- **Definition:** to perceive with the eyes; to understand
- **תרגום:** לראות; להבין
- **Example 1:** I can **see** the mountains from my window.
- **Example 2:** I **see** what you mean now.
- **Synonyms:** look at, observe; understand
- **Antonyms:** miss, ignore
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: see/sees/saw/seen.

### 7. **look** (v.)
- **Definition:** to direct your eyes somewhere; to appear
- **תרגום:** להסתכל; להיראות
- **Example 1:** **Look** at the beautiful sunset!
- **Example 2:** You **look** tired today.
- **Synonyms:** glance, appear, seem
- **Antonyms:** ignore
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל חשוב: **see** = תופס בעין (פסיבי), **look** = מכוון מבט (אקטיבי).

### 8. **watch** (v.)
- **Definition:** to look at something for a period of time
- **תרגום:** לצפות, לשמור על
- **Example 1:** We **watched** a great movie last night.
- **Example 2:** Please **watch** my bag while I use the bathroom.
- **Synonyms:** observe, view
- **Antonyms:** ignore
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **look at** = רגע קצר; **watch** = לאורך זמן.

### 9. **hear** (v.)
- **Definition:** to perceive with the ears
- **תרגום:** לשמוע
- **Example 1:** Did you **hear** that strange noise?
- **Example 2:** I **heard** the news this morning.
- **Synonyms:** listen, detect
- **Antonyms:** ignore
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **hear** = תופס באוזן; **listen** = מקשיב במכוון. Forms: hear/hears/heard/heard.

### 10. **listen** (v.)
- **Definition:** to pay attention to sounds
- **תרגום:** להקשיב, להאזין
- **Example 1:** **Listen** carefully to the instructions.
- **Example 2:** She loves to **listen** to classical music.
- **Synonyms:** hear attentively
- **Antonyms:** ignore
- **💡 Memory tip:** `listen **to**` (עם `to`). "Listen music" = שגוי.

### 11. **say** (v.)
- **Definition:** to speak words; to state
- **תרגום:** לומר
- **Example 1:** What did you **say**?
- **Example 2:** She **said** she was tired.
- **Synonyms:** state, mention, declare
- **Antonyms:** (none)
- **💡 Memory tip:** `say to someone`. Forms: say/says/said/said.

### 12. **tell** (v.)
- **Definition:** to communicate information to someone
- **תרגום:** לספר, להגיד ל-
- **Example 1:** **Tell** me about your day.
- **Example 2:** He **told** her the truth.
- **Synonyms:** inform, relate
- **Antonyms:** hide, conceal
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **say** = "say that" (ללא אובייקט מיידי); **tell** = "tell someone" (עם אובייקט). Forms: tell/tells/told/told.

### 13. **speak** (v.)
- **Definition:** to use the voice to produce words
- **תרגום:** לדבר
- **Example 1:** Do you **speak** English?
- **Example 2:** The president will **speak** at 8 PM.
- **Synonyms:** talk, converse
- **Antonyms:** be silent
- **💡 Memory tip:** `speak **a language**`. Forms: speak/speaks/spoke/spoken.

### 14. **talk** (v.)
- **Definition:** to speak in conversation
- **תרגום:** לדבר (שיחה)
- **Example 1:** We **talked** for hours on the phone.
- **Example 2:** Can we **talk** about this later?
- **Synonyms:** converse, chat
- **Antonyms:** be silent
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **speak** = פורמלי יותר; **talk** = שיחה יומיומית. `talk **to/with** someone`.

### 15. **think** (v.)
- **Definition:** to use your mind; to have an opinion
- **תרגום:** לחשוב
- **Example 1:** Let me **think** about it.
- **Example 2:** I **think** you're right.
- **Synonyms:** consider, believe
- **Antonyms:** (none direct)
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: think/thinks/thought/thought. `think about X` = לחשוב על X.

### 16. **know** (v.)
- **Definition:** to have information; to be familiar with
- **תרגום:** לדעת, להכיר
- **Example 1:** I **know** the answer.
- **Example 2:** Do you **know** my sister?
- **Synonyms:** understand, recognize
- **Antonyms:** not know, be unaware
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: know/knows/knew/known. "K" שקט!

### 17. **understand** (v.)
- **Definition:** to grasp the meaning of something
- **תרגום:** להבין
- **Example 1:** I don't **understand** this question.
- **Example 2:** She **understood** the lesson quickly.
- **Synonyms:** comprehend, grasp
- **Antonyms:** misunderstand
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: understand/understands/understood/understood.

### 18. **learn** (v.)
- **Definition:** to acquire knowledge or skill
- **תרגום:** ללמוד
- **Example 1:** I want to **learn** how to cook.
- **Example 2:** Children **learn** languages easily.
- **Synonyms:** study, discover
- **Antonyms:** forget, unlearn
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **learn** = לרכוש ידע (תהליך פעיל); **study** = לחקור (בתהליך מסודר).

### 19. **study** (v.)
- **Definition:** to examine carefully; to pursue academic learning
- **תרגום:** ללמוד (במסגרת מסודרת), לחקור
- **Example 1:** She **studies** biology at university.
- **Example 2:** I need to **study** for the test tomorrow.
- **Synonyms:** research, examine, learn
- **Antonyms:** ignore
- **💡 Memory tip:** באנגלית אקדמית, **study** הוא פועל חשוב: "studies show that...".

### 20. **teach** (v.)
- **Definition:** to give instruction to someone
- **תרגום:** ללמד, להורות
- **Example 1:** My mother **teaches** math.
- **Example 2:** He **taught** me how to drive.
- **Synonyms:** instruct, educate
- **Antonyms:** learn (opposite direction)
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: teach/teaches/taught/taught.

### 21. **read** (v.)
- **Definition:** to look at written words and understand them
- **תרגום:** לקרוא
- **Example 1:** I **read** a book every week.
- **Example 2:** She **read** the newspaper this morning.
- **Synonyms:** study, peruse
- **Antonyms:** (none direct)
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: read/reads/**read**/read. בעבר, המילה כתובה זהה אבל הגיית "red"!

### 22. **write** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make letters or words on a surface
- **תרגום:** לכתוב
- **Example 1:** Please **write** your name here.
- **Example 2:** She **wrote** a long letter to her friend.
- **Synonyms:** compose, record
- **Antonyms:** erase, delete
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: write/writes/wrote/written.

### 23. **eat** (v.)
- **Definition:** to consume food
- **תרגום:** לאכול
- **Example 1:** What do you want to **eat** for dinner?
- **Example 2:** We **ate** pizza last night.
- **Synonyms:** consume, devour
- **Antonyms:** starve, fast
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: eat/eats/ate/eaten.

### 24. **drink** (v.)
- **Definition:** to consume a liquid
- **תרגום:** לשתות
- **Example 1:** **Drink** plenty of water during exercise.
- **Example 2:** He **drank** three cups of coffee.
- **Synonyms:** sip, swallow
- **Antonyms:** (none direct)
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: drink/drinks/drank/drunk.

### 25. **sleep** (v.)
- **Definition:** to rest with closed eyes in unconsciousness
- **תרגום:** לישון
- **Example 1:** I **sleep** for eight hours every night.
- **Example 2:** The baby **slept** peacefully.
- **Synonyms:** rest, slumber
- **Antonyms:** wake up, be awake
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: sleep/sleeps/slept/slept.

### 26. **wake** (v.) / **wake up**
- **Definition:** to stop sleeping
- **תרגום:** להתעורר
- **Example 1:** I usually **wake up** at 7 AM.
- **Example 2:** The noise **woke** the baby.
- **Synonyms:** arise, rise
- **Antonyms:** sleep, fall asleep
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: wake/wakes/woke/woken.

### 27. **work** (v.)
- **Definition:** to do a job; to function properly
- **תרגום:** לעבוד; לפעול
- **Example 1:** She **works** at a hospital.
- **Example 2:** My computer doesn't **work**.
- **Synonyms:** labor, function, operate
- **Antonyms:** rest; break down
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: "I have a lot of work."

### 28. **play** (v.)
- **Definition:** to engage in a game; to perform music
- **תרגום:** לשחק; לנגן
- **Example 1:** The children are **playing** in the park.
- **Example 2:** She **plays** the piano beautifully.
- **Synonyms:** enjoy, perform
- **Antonyms:** work
- **💡 Memory tip:** `play a sport/game`, `play an instrument`.

### 29. **run** (v.)
- **Definition:** to move fast on foot; to operate
- **תרגום:** לרוץ; להפעיל
- **Example 1:** He **runs** five miles every morning.
- **Example 2:** She **runs** her own business.
- **Synonyms:** sprint, manage
- **Antonyms:** walk; stop
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: run/runs/ran/run.

### 30. **walk** (v.)
- **Definition:** to move on foot at a normal pace
- **תרגום:** ללכת, לצעוד
- **Example 1:** I **walk** to work every day.
- **Example 2:** Let's **walk** in the park.
- **Synonyms:** stroll, stride
- **Antonyms:** run, ride
- **💡 Memory tip:** Regular verb: walk/walked/walked.

## 🟢 Section A2: פעלים יומיומיים נוספים (31-60)

### 31. **sit** (v.)
- **Definition:** to rest on your bottom
- **תרגום:** לשבת
- **Example 1:** Please **sit** down.
- **Example 2:** We **sat** by the fire all evening.
- **Synonyms:** be seated
- **Antonyms:** stand, rise
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: sit/sits/sat/sat.

### 32. **stand** (v.)
- **Definition:** to be on your feet in an upright position
- **תרגום:** לעמוד
- **Example 1:** She **stood** by the window for hours.
- **Example 2:** Please **stand** when the judge enters.
- **Synonyms:** rise, be upright
- **Antonyms:** sit, lie
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: stand/stands/stood/stood.

### 33. **live** (v.)
- **Definition:** to be alive; to reside somewhere
- **תרגום:** לחיות; לגור
- **Example 1:** We **live** in Tel Aviv.
- **Example 2:** My grandfather **lived** to be 95.
- **Synonyms:** reside, exist, dwell
- **Antonyms:** die
- **💡 Memory tip:** pronunciation: /lɪv/ (פועל) vs. /laɪv/ (live, תואר = חי).

### 34. **die** (v.)
- **Definition:** to stop living
- **תרגום:** למות
- **Example 1:** The plant **died** because I forgot to water it.
- **Example 2:** She's afraid to **die** alone.
- **Synonyms:** pass away, perish
- **Antonyms:** live, survive
- **💡 Memory tip:** -ing form: dying (not dieing!).

### 35. **love** (v.)
- **Definition:** to have deep affection for
- **תרגום:** לאהוב
- **Example 1:** I **love** my family.
- **Example 2:** She **loves** chocolate.
- **Synonyms:** adore, cherish
- **Antonyms:** hate, dislike
- **💡 Memory tip:** גם שם עצם: "love is beautiful".

### 36. **like** (v.)
- **Definition:** to find something enjoyable
- **תרגום:** לאהוב (פחות חזק מ-love)
- **Example 1:** I **like** your new dress.
- **Example 2:** She **likes** playing tennis.
- **Synonyms:** enjoy, fancy
- **Antonyms:** dislike, hate
- **💡 Memory tip:** `like + V-ing` (like playing) או `like + to V` (like to play).

### 37. **want** (v.)
- **Definition:** to desire something
- **תרגום:** לרצות
- **Example 1:** I **want** to go home.
- **Example 2:** Do you **want** some coffee?
- **Synonyms:** wish, desire
- **Antonyms:** refuse
- **💡 Memory tip:** `want + to V` (want to go), `want + noun` (want a car).

### 38. **need** (v.)
- **Definition:** to require something; to have a necessity
- **תרגום:** להזדקק, להצטרך
- **Example 1:** We **need** more time to finish this.
- **Example 2:** The plant **needs** water.
- **Synonyms:** require, demand
- **Antonyms:** (none)
- **💡 Memory tip:** הבדל: **want** = רוצה (not necessarily needed); **need** = מצטרך.

### 39. **give** (v.)
- **Definition:** to hand over; to provide
- **תרגום:** לתת
- **Example 1:** She **gave** me a book for my birthday.
- **Example 2:** Please **give** me a moment.
- **Synonyms:** hand, provide, donate
- **Antonyms:** take, receive
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: give/gives/gave/given.

### 40. **take** (v.)
- **Definition:** to remove; to accept; to carry
- **תרגום:** לקחת
- **Example 1:** **Take** this umbrella with you.
- **Example 2:** I **took** the first bus.
- **Synonyms:** grab, accept, carry
- **Antonyms:** give, return
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: take/takes/took/taken. פועל רב-שימושי עם הרבה phrasal verbs.

### 41. **get** (v.)
- **Definition:** to obtain; to become; to receive
- **תרגום:** לקבל; להיעשות; להגיע
- **Example 1:** I **got** a new phone yesterday.
- **Example 2:** It's **getting** cold outside.
- **Synonyms:** receive, obtain, become
- **Antonyms:** lose, give
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: get/gets/got/gotten (American) / got (British). פועל הכי רב-שימושי באנגלית!

### 42. **put** (v.)
- **Definition:** to place in a position
- **תרגום:** לשים, להניח
- **Example 1:** **Put** the book on the table.
- **Example 2:** He **put** his shoes by the door.
- **Synonyms:** place, set
- **Antonyms:** remove, take away
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: put/puts/put/put. לא משתנה בעבר!

### 43. **make** (v.)
- **Definition:** to create; to cause something to happen
- **תרגום:** לעשות, ליצור
- **Example 1:** She **made** a cake for the party.
- **Example 2:** This news **makes** me happy.
- **Synonyms:** create, produce, cause
- **Antonyms:** destroy, break
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: make/makes/made/made. מבלבל עם "do" - **make** = ליצור, **do** = לבצע.

### 44. **let** (v.)
- **Definition:** to allow; to permit
- **תרגום:** לתת, לאפשר
- **Example 1:** **Let** me help you.
- **Example 2:** My parents **let** me stay out late.
- **Synonyms:** allow, permit
- **Antonyms:** forbid, prevent
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: let/lets/let/let. `let someone V` = מאפשר ל-X לעשות V.

### 45. **help** (v.)
- **Definition:** to give support; to assist
- **תרגום:** לעזור
- **Example 1:** Can you **help** me with this?
- **Example 2:** She **helped** her friend move.
- **Synonyms:** assist, aid, support
- **Antonyms:** hinder, obstruct
- **💡 Memory tip:** `help someone [to] do something`. ה-"to" אופציונלי!

### 46. **try** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make an effort; to test something
- **תרגום:** לנסות
- **Example 1:** **Try** your best on the exam.
- **Example 2:** I **tried** the new restaurant last night.
- **Synonyms:** attempt, test
- **Antonyms:** give up
- **💡 Memory tip:** `try to V` = לעשות מאמץ; `try V-ing` = לנסות בתור ניסיון.

### 47. **start** (v.)
- **Definition:** to begin
- **תרגום:** להתחיל
- **Example 1:** The movie **starts** at 8.
- **Example 2:** I **started** learning English two years ago.
- **Synonyms:** begin, commence, initiate
- **Antonyms:** finish, end, stop
- **💡 Memory tip:** `start` = `begin` - ניתן להחליף ביניהם.

### 48. **begin** (v.)
- **Definition:** to start
- **תרגום:** להתחיל
- **Example 1:** When does the class **begin**?
- **Example 2:** She **began** to cry.
- **Synonyms:** start, commence
- **Antonyms:** end, finish
- **💡 Memory tip:** Forms: begin/begins/began/begun. קצת פורמלי יותר מ-"start".

### 49. **stop** (v.)
- **Definition:** to cease; to prevent from continuing
- **תרגום:** להפסיק
- **Example 1:** **Stop** talking, please.
- **Example 2:** The rain finally **stopped**.
- **Synonyms:** cease, halt, quit
- **Antonyms:** start, continue
- **💡 Memory tip:** `stop V-ing` = הפסקת עשייה. `stop to V` = עצירה כדי לעשות.

### 50. **finish** (v.)
- **Definition:** to complete; to bring to an end
- **תרגום:** לסיים, לגמור
- **Example 1:** I **finished** my homework at 10.
- **Example 2:** Let's **finish** this project today.
- **Synonyms:** complete, end, conclude
- **Antonyms:** start, begin
- **💡 Memory tip:** `finish V-ing` = `finish doing X` (לא "finish to do").

### 51. **continue** (v.)
- **Definition:** to keep doing something

- **Example 2:** She **continued** studying until midnight.
- **Synonyms:** carry on, proceed, keep going
- **Antonyms:** stop, halt
- **💡 Memory tip:** `continue V-ing` או `continue to V` - שניהם נכונים.

### 52. **open** (v.)

### 53. **close** (v.)

### 54. **buy** (v.)
- **Definition:** to obtain by paying money
- **תרגום:** לקנות
- **Example 1:** I want to **buy** a new car.
- **Example 2:** She **bought** some vegetables at the market.

### 55. **sell** (v.)

- **תרגום:** למכור
- **Example 1:** They **sell** used books here.
- **Example 2:** He **sold** his old bike.

- פעלים בסיסיים ליומיום (be, have, do, go, come)

## 📚 לקחים חשובים מהחלק הזה:

### 1. **הבדלים בין זוגות מילים דומות**
- `see` vs `look` (פסיבי vs אקטיבי)
- `hear` vs `listen` (פסיבי vs אקטיבי)
- `say` vs `tell` (ללא/עם אובייקט)
- `rise` vs `raise` (intransitive vs transitive)

### 2. **פעלים עם מילות יחס חובה**
- `listen **to**`
- `look **at**`
- `wait **for**`
- `agree **with/on**`
- `depend **on**`

### 3. **פעלים עם `V-ing` ו-`to V`**
- `enjoy + V-ing` (לא "to V"!)
- `want + to V`
- `stop + V-ing` (stop doing X)
- `try + V-ing` (try doing X = נסה לעשות כחלופה)
