# 📊 קטגוריה 1: רמות וכמויות (20 מילים)

### 851. **significant** (adj.)
- **Definition:** important, noticeable
- **תרגום:** משמעותי
- **Example 1:** There's a **significant** difference.
- **Example 2:** She made a **significant** contribution.
- **Synonyms:** important, notable
- **Antonyms:** insignificant, trivial
- **💡 Memory tip:** `significant change/difference/increase` - Collocations.

### 852. **essential** (adj.)
- **Definition:** absolutely necessary
- **תרגום:** חיוני
- **Example 1:** Water is **essential** for life.
- **Example 2:** It's **essential** that you attend.
- **Synonyms:** necessary, crucial, vital
- **Antonyms:** unnecessary, optional
- **💡 Memory tip:** `essence` (שם עצם) = מהות.
