## Section A: Verbs (פעלים) – 30 מילים דוגמה

### 1. **achieve** (v.)
- **Definition:** to successfully reach or accomplish a goal through effort
- **תרגום:** להשיג, להצליח להגיע אל
- **Example 1:** She worked hard for years to **achieve** her dream of becoming a doctor.
- **Example 2:** The team **achieved** remarkable results in the final quarter.
- **Synonyms:** accomplish, attain, reach
- **Antonyms:** fail, miss
- **💡 Memory tip:** From Old French *achever* ("to finish"). Think: "A-chief (leader) achieves things."

### 2. **acquire** (v.)
- **Definition:** to come to own or possess something, often through effort
- **תרגום:** לרכוש, לקנות, להשיג
- **Example 1:** Over the years, the museum **acquired** a vast collection of rare paintings.
- **Example 2:** Children **acquire** language skills naturally in their early years.
- **Synonyms:** obtain, gain, procure
- **Antonyms:** lose, forfeit
- **💡 Memory tip:** Think "a-cure" – you acquire the cure.

### 3. **analyze** (v.)
- **Definition:** to examine something in detail in order to understand it
- **תרגום:** לנתח
- **Example 1:** Scientists must **analyze** the data carefully before reaching a conclusion.
- **Example 2:** The teacher asked her students to **analyze** the poem's structure.
- **Synonyms:** examine, investigate, study
- **Antonyms:** synthesize, combine
- **💡 Memory tip:** "Anal-ysis" – looking at the small parts of something.

### 4. **approach** (v.)
- **Definition:** to come near to; to begin to deal with something
- **תרגום:** להתקרב אל; לגשת אל (נושא)
- **Example 1:** As winter **approaches**, the days become shorter.
- **Example 2:** We need a new way to **approach** this problem.
- **Synonyms:** near, tackle, address
- **Antonyms:** retreat, avoid

### 5. **argue** (v.)
- **Definition:** to give reasons for or against something; to disagree verbally
- **תרגום:** לטעון; להתווכח
- **Example 1:** Many economists **argue** that inflation will rise next year.
- **Example 2:** The couple **argued** about money for hours.
- **Synonyms:** claim, contend, debate, quarrel
- **Antonyms:** agree, concur

### 6. **assume** (v.)
- **Definition:** to believe something is true without proof; to take on a role or responsibility
- **תרגום:** להניח; לקבל על עצמו (תפקיד)
- **Example 1:** I **assumed** you were coming to the meeting.
- **Example 2:** She will **assume** the role of director next month.
- **Synonyms:** presume, suppose, undertake
- **Antonyms:** doubt, question

### 7. **attempt** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make an effort to do something
- **תרגום:** לנסות, לעשות מאמץ
- **Example 1:** He **attempted** to climb the mountain but had to turn back.
- **Example 2:** The students will **attempt** the final exam tomorrow.
- **Synonyms:** try, endeavor, strive
- **Antonyms:** give up, abandon

### 8. **avoid** (v.)
- **Definition:** to stay away from; to prevent something from happening
- **תרגום:** להימנע מ-
- **Example 1:** You should **avoid** junk food if you want to stay healthy.
- **Example 2:** The driver swerved to **avoid** the dog.
- **Synonyms:** evade, escape, dodge
- **Antonyms:** confront, face, encounter

### 9. **believe** (v.)
- **Definition:** to accept as true; to have faith in
- **תרגום:** להאמין
- **Example 1:** Scientists do not **believe** that theory is supported by evidence.
- **Example 2:** She **believes** in the power of education.
- **Synonyms:** trust, accept, think
- **Antonyms:** doubt, disbelieve, reject

### 10. **create** (v.)
- **Definition:** to bring something into existence; to produce
- **תרגום:** ליצור, לברוא
- **Example 1:** The artist **created** a masterpiece in just three days.
- **Example 2:** New technology is **creating** jobs in many industries.
- **Synonyms:** produce, generate, form, make
- **Antonyms:** destroy, demolish

### 11. **decide** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make a choice after thinking
- **תרגום:** להחליט
- **Example 1:** We haven't **decided** where to go on vacation yet.
- **Example 2:** The judge will **decide** the case next week.
- **Synonyms:** determine, choose, resolve
- **Antonyms:** hesitate, waver

### 12. **describe** (v.)
- **Definition:** to say what someone or something is like
- **תרגום:** לתאר
- **Example 1:** Can you **describe** the man you saw?
- **Example 2:** The article **describes** how the disease spreads.
- **Synonyms:** portray, depict, explain
- **Antonyms:** (no direct antonym)

### 13. **develop** (v.)
- **Definition:** to grow or cause to grow and become more advanced
- **תרגום:** לפתח, להתפתח
- **Example 1:** The country has **developed** rapidly over the past decade.
- **Example 2:** She **developed** a new method for testing water quality.
- **Synonyms:** grow, evolve, advance, progress
- **Antonyms:** regress, decline, deteriorate

### 14. **discover** (v.)
- **Definition:** to find something new, especially a place, fact, or phenomenon
- **תרגום:** לגלות
- **Example 1:** Columbus is said to have **discovered** America in 1492.
- **Example 2:** Researchers **discovered** a new species of fish in the deep ocean.
- **Synonyms:** find, uncover, reveal, detect
- **Antonyms:** hide, conceal, overlook

### 15. **encourage** (v.)
- **Definition:** to give someone support, confidence, or hope
- **תרגום:** לעודד
- **Example 1:** My parents always **encouraged** me to read.
- **Example 2:** These rewards are designed to **encourage** good behavior.
- **Synonyms:** motivate, inspire, support
- **Antonyms:** discourage, dissuade

### 16. **establish** (v.)
- **Definition:** to set up or create something that is meant to last
- **תרגום:** לייסד, לבסס
- **Example 1:** The university was **established** in 1925.
- **Example 2:** The company has **established** a strong reputation in the field.
- **Synonyms:** found, create, institute
- **Antonyms:** abolish, dissolve

### 17. **exist** (v.)
- **Definition:** to be present in the real world; to live
- **תרגום:** להתקיים, להיות קיים
- **Example 1:** Many rare species still **exist** in the Amazon rainforest.
- **Example 2:** The theory suggests that parallel universes may **exist**.
- **Synonyms:** be, live, remain
- **Antonyms:** vanish, cease

### 18. **expect** (v.)
- **Definition:** to believe something will happen
- **תרגום:** לצפות, לחכות ל-
- **Example 1:** We **expect** the rain to stop by evening.
- **Example 2:** She didn't **expect** such a warm welcome.
- **Synonyms:** anticipate, predict, foresee
- **Antonyms:** be surprised by

### 19. **explain** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make something clear by describing it
- **תרגום:** להסביר
- **Example 1:** Could you **explain** the rules of the game?
- **Example 2:** He **explained** why he was late.
- **Synonyms:** clarify, illustrate, describe
- **Antonyms:** confuse, mystify

### 20. **explore** (v.)
- **Definition:** to travel through a place to learn about it; to examine an idea
- **תרגום:** לחקור, לתור
- **Example 1:** The team set out to **explore** the unknown cave.
- **Example 2:** The book **explores** the question of free will.
- **Synonyms:** investigate, examine, survey
- **Antonyms:** ignore, overlook

### 21. **grow** (v.)
- **Definition:** to become larger or develop
- **תרגום:** לגדול, לצמוח
- **Example 1:** The plant **grew** three inches in one week.
- **Example 2:** The company has **grown** rapidly since 2020.
- **Synonyms:** increase, expand, develop
- **Antonyms:** shrink, decline

### 22. **happen** (v.)
- **Definition:** to take place; to occur
- **תרגום:** לקרות, להתרחש
- **Example 1:** Nothing unusual **happened** yesterday.
- **Example 2:** Can you tell me what **happened** at the meeting?
- **Synonyms:** occur, take place, arise
- **Antonyms:** (no direct antonym)

### 23. **improve** (v.)
- **Definition:** to make or become better
- **תרגום:** לשפר, להשתפר
- **Example 1:** Practice will help you **improve** your English.
- **Example 2:** The weather should **improve** tomorrow.
- **Synonyms:** enhance, upgrade, refine
- **Antonyms:** worsen, deteriorate

### 24. **include** (v.)
- **Definition:** to have as part of a whole
- **תרגום:** לכלול
- **Example 1:** The price **includes** breakfast.
- **Example 2:** The report **includes** several important recommendations.
- **Synonyms:** contain, comprise, incorporate
- **Antonyms:** exclude, omit

### 25. **increase** (v.)
- **Definition:** to become greater in number or amount
- **תרגום:** להגדיל, לעלות
- **Example 1:** The population of the city has **increased** sharply.
- **Example 2:** Exercise **increases** your energy levels.
- **Synonyms:** rise, grow, expand
- **Antonyms:** decrease, reduce, diminish

### 26. **learn** (v.)
- **Definition:** to gain knowledge or skill
- **תרגום:** ללמוד
- **Example 1:** Children **learn** languages more easily than adults.
- **Example 2:** I **learned** about the news this morning.
- **Synonyms:** study, master, acquire
- **Antonyms:** forget, unlearn

### 27. **occur** (v.)
- **Definition:** to happen, especially without being planned
- **תרגום:** לקרות, להתרחש (פורמלי יותר)
- **Example 1:** The accident **occurred** at 3 a.m.
- **Example 2:** Earthquakes often **occur** along fault lines.
- **Synonyms:** happen, take place, arise
- **Antonyms:** (no direct antonym)
- **💡 Memory tip:** More formal/academic than "happen" – use in reading comprehension texts.

### 28. **prove** (v.)
- **Definition:** to show that something is true with evidence
- **תרגום:** להוכיח
- **Example 1:** The test **proved** that the water was safe to drink.
- **Example 2:** Can you **prove** your claim?
- **Synonyms:** demonstrate, confirm, verify
- **Antonyms:** disprove, refute

### 29. **recognize** (v.)
- **Definition:** to know someone or something because you have seen them before; to accept as true
- **תרגום:** לזהות; להכיר ב-
- **Example 1:** I didn't **recognize** him with his new haircut.
- **Example 2:** The company **recognized** her hard work with a promotion.
- **Synonyms:** identify, acknowledge
- **Antonyms:** ignore, overlook

### 30. **suggest** (v.)
- **Definition:** to propose an idea; to indicate something without saying it directly
- **תרגום:** להציע; לרמוז
- **Example 1:** I **suggest** that we meet at 7 p.m.
- **Example 2:** The results **suggest** that the treatment is effective.
- **Synonyms:** propose, recommend, imply
- **Antonyms:** demand, insist

## Section B: Nouns (שמות עצם) – 40 מילים דוגמה

- **Antonyms:** disadvantage, drawback

### 33. **attempt** (n.)
- **Definition:** an effort to do something
- **תרגום:** ניסיון, מאמץ
- **Example:** This is my third **attempt** to pass the test.
- **Synonyms:** effort, try, endeavor
- **Antonyms:** abandonment

### 34. **behavior** (n.)
- **Definition:** the way a person or animal acts
- **תרגום:** התנהגות
- **Example:** The teacher praised the students' **behavior** during the trip.
- **Synonyms:** conduct, manner, actions

### 35. **benefit** (n.)
- **Definition:** an advantage or gain
- **תרגום:** תועלת, הטבה
- **Example:** Regular exercise has many health **benefits**.
- **Synonyms:** advantage, gain, profit
- **Antonyms:** disadvantage, harm

### 36. **challenge** (n.)
- **Definition:** a difficult task or situation
- **תרגום:** אתגר
- **Example:** Learning a new language is a real **challenge**.
- **Synonyms:** difficulty, task, test
- **Antonyms:** ease, simplicity

### 37. **consequence** (n.)
- **Definition:** a result or effect of an action
- **תרגום:** תוצאה, השלכה
- **Example:** Climate change has serious **consequences** for our planet.
- **Synonyms:** result, outcome, effect
- **Antonyms:** cause
- **💡 Memory tip:** Usually refers to NEGATIVE results. Positive results = "benefits" or "outcomes."

### 38. **decision** (n.)
- **Definition:** a choice made after thinking
- **תרגום:** החלטה
- **Example:** Making the right **decision** can change your life.
- **Synonyms:** choice, judgment, resolution
- **Antonyms:** indecision, hesitation

### 39. **difference** (n.)
- **Definition:** a way in which things are not the same
- **תרגום:** הבדל
- **Example:** What is the **difference** between these two theories?
- **Synonyms:** distinction, contrast
- **Antonyms:** similarity, resemblance

### 40. **effect** (n.)
- **Definition:** a result; the change caused by something
- **תרגום:** השפעה, אפקט
- **Example:** The new law had a positive **effect** on the economy.
- **Synonyms:** result, outcome, consequence
- **Antonyms:** cause, source
- **⚠️ Don't confuse with:** "affect" (verb, to influence) vs. "effect" (noun, a result). "The weather affects my mood" vs. "The effect of the weather is clear."

### 41. **evidence** (n.)
- **Definition:** information that shows something is true
- **תרגום:** הוכחה, ראיה
- **Example:** There is strong **evidence** that the climate is changing.
- **Synonyms:** proof, data, facts
- **Antonyms:** refutation

### 42. **example** (n.)
- **Definition:** something that shows what a thing is like
- **תרגום:** דוגמה
- **Example:** Can you give me an **example** of a chemical reaction?
- **Synonyms:** instance, illustration, case

### 43. **experience** (n.)
- **Definition:** knowledge or skill gained over time; an event that happens to someone
- **תרגום:** ניסיון, חוויה
- **Example:** She has ten years of **experience** as a nurse.

- **Antonyms:** theoretical, impractical

### 85. **recent** (adj.)
- **Definition:** having happened a short time ago
- **תרגום:** אחרון, מהזמן האחרון
- **Example:** **Recent** studies suggest that coffee may be good for health.
- **Synonyms:** new, fresh, latest
- **Antonyms:** old, ancient, outdated

### 86. **significant** (adj.)
- **Definition:** important or meaningful
- **תרגום:** משמעותי, חשוב
- **Example:** There has been a **significant** decrease in unemployment.
- **Synonyms:** important, notable, considerable
- **Antonyms:** insignificant, minor, trivial

### 87. **similar** (adj.)
- **Definition:** almost the same
- **תרגום:** דומה
- **Example:** The two paintings are **similar** but not identical.
- **Synonyms:** alike, comparable, like
- **Antonyms:** different, dissimilar, opposite

### 88. **successful** (adj.)
- **Definition:** achieving what was wanted
- **תרגום:** מצליח
- **Example:** She is a **successful** lawyer and author.
- **Synonyms:** prosperous, triumphant, accomplished
- **Antonyms:** unsuccessful, failed

### 89. **true** (adj.)
- **Definition:** in agreement with facts; real
- **תרגום:** נכון, אמיתי
- **Example:** Is it **true** that you're moving to Canada?
- **Synonyms:** real, accurate, correct
- **Antonyms:** false, untrue, fake

### 90. **useful** (adj.)
- **Definition:** helpful; producing good results
- **תרגום:** שימושי, מועיל
- **Example:** A dictionary is a **useful** tool for language learners.
- **Synonyms:** helpful, practical, beneficial
- **Antonyms:** useless, pointless

## Section D: Adverbs & Connectives (תארי פועל ומילות חיבור) – 10 מילים דוגמה

### 91. **however** (adv./conj.)
- **Definition:** used to introduce a contrast
- **תרגום:** אולם, עם זאת
- **Example:** The plan sounds good. **However**, it will be expensive.
- **Synonyms:** nevertheless, nonetheless, yet, but
- **💡 Tip:** Very common in academic writing to show contrast.

### 92. **therefore** (adv.)
- **Definition:** for that reason; as a result
- **תרגום:** לכן, לפיכך
- **Example:** It was raining; **therefore**, we stayed inside.
- **Synonyms:** thus, consequently, so, hence
- **💡 Tip:** Used to show logical conclusions in reading passages.

### 93. **although** (conj.)
- **Definition:** despite the fact that
- **תרגום:** אם כי, למרות ש-
- **Example:** **Although** it was cold, we went for a walk.
- **Synonyms:** though, even though, despite the fact that
- **Antonyms:** because

### 94. **moreover** (adv.)
- **Definition:** in addition; also
- **תרגום:** יתרה מכך, בנוסף
- **Example:** The plan is expensive. **Moreover**, it's risky.
- **Synonyms:** furthermore, additionally, besides
- **💡 Tip:** Formal/academic style – a favorite in reading comprehension texts.

### 95. **consequently** (adv.)
- **Definition:** as a result
- **תרגום:** כתוצאה מכך
- **Example:** She studied hard; **consequently**, she passed the exam.
- **Synonyms:** therefore, thus, as a result
- **Antonyms:** (shows cause/effect)

### 96. **nevertheless** (adv.)
- **Definition:** in spite of what has just been said
- **תרגום:** בכל זאת, אף על פי כן
- **Example:** The team lost; **nevertheless**, they played well.
- **Synonyms:** however, nonetheless, still
- **Antonyms:** (shows contrast)

### 97. **furthermore** (adv.)
- **Definition:** in addition
- **תרגום:** יתר על כן
- **Example:** The food was delicious. **Furthermore**, the service was excellent.
- **Synonyms:** moreover, also, besides, additionally

### 98. **despite** (prep.)
- **Definition:** without being affected by something
- **תרגום:** למרות
- **Example:** **Despite** the rain, the game continued.
- **Synonyms:** in spite of, notwithstanding
- **💡 Grammar note:** "Despite" is followed by a noun or gerund, NOT a full clause. Use "although" before a clause: "Although it rained, …" vs. "Despite the rain, …"

### 99. **whereas** (conj.)
- **Definition:** in contrast; while
- **תרגום:** בעוד ש-, לעומת זאת
- **Example:** He likes coffee, **whereas** his wife prefers tea.
- **Synonyms:** while, on the other hand

### 100. **eventually** (adv.)
- **Definition:** in the end; after a long time
- **תרגום:** בסופו של דבר
- **Example:** She **eventually** became a famous scientist.
- **Synonyms:** finally, ultimately, at last
- **Antonyms:** immediately
- **⚠️ Common mistake:** "Eventually" means "in the end" in English – NOT "possibly." ("possibly" = "maybe.")
